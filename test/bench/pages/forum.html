Ask Forum: Do you read foreign-language pages with side-by-side translation?

412 points by polyglot_pat 14 hours ago | hide | past | favorite | 150 comments

I have been using a split-view translator for a few weeks: the original page on the left, the translation on the right, scrolled in sync. It translates only what is on screen, which keeps the bill small, but I keep wondering how much is still wasted on repeated interface text.

Curious how others handle this. Do you translate whole pages up front, or only the parts you actually read? And does anyone measure the token cost?
